Adding a song from rehearsal
  1. Record the run-through in Voice Memos.
  2. In Voice Memos tap ⋯ → Save to Files.
  3. Tap Add song above and pick that file.
  4. Check the BPM, tap the waveform where the groove you want starts, press Check, then Save.

Songs are stored in Safari on this phone only. Safari can clear data from sites you haven't opened in 7 days, so open this page every few days and keep your Voice Memos as a backup. Turn the silent switch off and plug your IEM in by cable, not Bluetooth.
