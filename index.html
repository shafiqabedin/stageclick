The backup has every gig's songs, BPMs and notes. Recordings are not included.

Everything is stored in Safari on this phone only. Safari can clear data from sites you haven't opened in 7 days, so open this page every few days and save a backup. Turn the silent switch off and plug your IEM in by cable, not Bluetooth.

Adding a song from rehearsal
  1. Record the run-through in Voice Memos.
  2. In Voice Memos tap ⋯ → Save to Files.
  3. Tap Add from a file above and pick that file.
  4. Check the BPM, tap the waveform where the groove you want starts, press Check, then Save.

Recording a practice or the gig
  1. Tap ● Start recording here, then go to Show and play as usual. The mic also measures the band's tempo.
  2. Each time you press Play or Click only on Show, a song marker is added.
  3. Keep the screen on and Stageclick open. It saves every 5 s and restarts itself if iOS drops the mic. A red alarm shows if the mic goes silent.
  4. Listen back here, then use Save to Files to keep it and Delete to free space.
